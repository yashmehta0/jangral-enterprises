About the Company

Two decades of protective & service clothing

A trusted Jammu-based manufacturer of defence, police, paramilitary and ordnance clothing — serving forces and institutions across India since 2006.

Founded in 2006 in Bishnah, Jammu, Jangral Enterprises began as a small-scale manufacturing unit and has grown into a dependable supplier of protective and service apparel for defence, police and paramilitary buyers.

Our work centres on the harness of bulletproof jackets and patkas, army and police uniforms, tactical and winter jackets, sleeping bags, kit bags and field gear — produced with reinforced construction, triple stitching and durable, mission-ready materials.

Every bulletproof vest is designed with high-strength construction — triple stitching, reinforced pockets and YKK zips — built to demanding standards. Our track suits use breathable, sweat-absorbent fabric, and our field kit is made to hold up in real service conditions.

Each order is handled personally, with a focus on quality, correct fit and on-time delivery — whether for a single institution or a bulk force-level requirement.
