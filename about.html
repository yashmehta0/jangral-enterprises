About the Company

Protective & service clothing, made in Jammu

An ISO 9001:2015 certified, MSME-registered manufacturer of defence, police, paramilitary and ordnance clothing — serving forces and institutions across India.

Established in 1990 in Bishnah, Jammu, Jangral Enterprises is a trusted manufacturer of protective and service apparel for defence, police and paramilitary buyers, grown from a small-scale unit into a dependable supplier across India.

Our work centres on the harness of bulletproof jackets, patkas and tactical vests, army and police uniforms, tactical and winter jackets, rainwear, sleeping bags, kit bags, tents and field kit — produced with reinforced construction, triple stitching and durable, mission-ready materials.

Every protective vest is built to demanding standards — triple stitching, reinforced pockets and YKK zips. Our track suits use breathable, sweat-absorbent fabric, and our field kit is made to hold up in real service conditions.

Each order is handled personally, with a focus on quality, correct fit and on-time delivery — whether for a single institution or a bulk force-level requirement.
